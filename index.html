Mission

Affordable tools for large-scale data.

Pazator builds infrastructure for surveillance, intelligence, and operational systems: tools that handle large data volumes at low cost, with your data kept where it belongs, in your hands.

We build for cases where enterprise vendors are not an option.

Approach

How we build.

01

Local-first by default

02

Affordable at scale

Built to ingest and analyze millions of records without enterprise licensing or pricing.

03

Air-gap ready

Deployable with no internet and no cloud dependency, built for networks where nothing is allowed to leave.
